About Lesotho

A mountain kingdom rich in culture, landscapes and community.

Welcome to the Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a landlocked country in southern Africa, known for its high altitude, mountain scenery and distinctive Basotho culture. Its landscapes invite visitors to explore nature, while its communities offer opportunities to learn about traditions, crafts, music and everyday life.

Tourism can support local businesses when visitors travel respectfully, use local guides where possible and protect the environment.

Travel respectfully

  • Ask permission before photographing people.
  • Follow local guidance and safety instructions.
  • Leave natural places clean and undamaged.
  • Support local craftspeople and businesses.

Did you know?

Lesotho is often called the “Kingdom in the Sky” because of its mountainous terrain and high elevation.

Traditional blankets, the mokorotlo hat, music and oral storytelling are recognised parts of Basotho cultural identity.

Depending on location and conditions, visitors may enjoy hiking, pony trekking, scenic drives and photography.
